In The Farmer Was Replaced, speed upgrades increase the movement speed of the farming drone and the execution speed of your Python-style code. 
The base operation time starts at 2.5 ms (about 400 ticks per second), and speed upgrades reduce tick time by a factor of 1.5,
 while Sunflower power can further double your speed


Speed Mechanics and Upgrades

• Base Operation Time: Starts at 2.5 ms (roughly 0.5 seconds for a 200-operation action like harvesting).
• Speed Unlocks: Each speed upgrade reduces operation time by a factor of 1.5 (e.g., Speed 1 makes ticks take 2/3 of normal time, or ~1.667 ms).
• Drone & Code Speed: Upgrading speed boosts both physical drone movement across the grid and how fast your scripts process commands.
